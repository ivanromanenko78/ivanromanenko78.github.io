# Ivan & Daniil — Version 2

A separate, warmer landing-page design for local Lisbon businesses. Version 1 remains unchanged in the parent directory.

- Portuguese: index.html
- English: en.html
- Russian: ru.html
- Offer: €400/month; one filming session up to 2h, two short videos up to 30s, four posts, four Stories, Portuguese copy, Instagram/Facebook publication, one revision round and brief report.
- No WebGL, external scripts, trackers or backend dependencies.
- The brief form prepares a message for the visitor to review and explicitly send through WhatsApp or copy. It does not claim that a message has been delivered.
- Team descriptions use supplied project information. Initial avatars represent the team; they are not artificial portraits. No clients, reviews or outcomes are invented.
- Illustrative photographs are not presented as agency work.
