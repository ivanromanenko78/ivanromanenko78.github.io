A CLEAR PROCESS

From our first conversation to your next post.

  1. Get to know your business

    We discuss your goals and agree on the scope, price, timeline and payment.

  2. Plan and film

    We prepare ideas and a filming plan, then visit for one session of up to 2 hours.

  3. Edit and approve

    We produce your videos and copy, collect one round of revisions and get your approval.

  4. Publish and review

    We publish the approved content, prepare a brief report and discuss the following month.

We review delivery and available performance data. We do not promise a fixed number of new customers or sales.
