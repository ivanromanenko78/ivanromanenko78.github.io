Reels or a corporate video: which does your business need?

The right format depends on where the video will appear and what you need to explain. Choose before you film.

By Ivan & Daniil · 04.11.2026 · 3 min read

01The question comes before the format02When to choose a Reel03When to choose a corporate video04One shoot can serve several channels05How we choose with you

The question comes before the format

Start with where the video will be watched. Someone scrolling Instagram has a different context from someone visiting your website to choose a supplier. The same business may need both formats, with different messages and pacing.

When to choose a Reel

A short vertical video is useful for demonstrating a process, introducing something new or answering a specific question. Focus each video on one idea and plan shots for a phone screen. A clear opening and readable captions help viewers follow, without guaranteeing reach or virality.

When to choose a corporate video

If you need to introduce your company, team, method and services with more context, a corporate video gives the story room. It can work on your website, in meetings or in presentations. Duration should follow the message and channel rather than a rule that business videos have to be long.

One shoot can serve several channels

You can plan shots and interviews for a main piece and short edits. Plan that before filming: framing, sound and session time affect the versions available. More cuts mean additional editing and need to be part of the quote.
