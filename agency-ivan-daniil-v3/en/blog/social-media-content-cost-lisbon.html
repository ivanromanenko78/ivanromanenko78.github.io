How much does social media content cost in Lisbon?

Compare the scope before comparing prices. What our €400 package includes and what is quoted separately.

By Ivan & Daniil · 04.11.2026 · 3 min read

01Start with the deliverables02Our starting point: €400 per month03What should be written down04Content and advertising are different jobs05How to decide whether it fits

Start with the deliverables

A monthly fee is only comparable when you know what it includes. Two proposals can both promise social media content while offering very different filming time, volumes and responsibilities. Before comparing prices, request a list of deliverables, formats and channels.

Our starting point: €400 per month

Ivan & Daniil’s package includes one visit for up to two hours of filming, two videos up to 30 seconds each, four posts and four Stories created from the session. It includes Portuguese copy, Instagram and Facebook publishing, one revision round and a brief report. The first month is a paid trial. This is our package price, not a market average.

What should be written down

The proposal confirms how publications are counted, whether content is adapted between networks, where filming takes place and the schedule. Approval deadlines, payment and VAT also need to be clear. A number of posts does not automatically mean the same number of separate productions.

Content and advertising are different jobs

Filming, editing and publishing are different from running paid campaigns. Ad setup, management and platform spend are separate. Customer messages and complex animation also need their own agreed scope.

How to decide whether it fits

Choose a specific objective for the first month: introduce your space, explain a service or establish a publishing routine. Compare the delivery against that goal and review the available metrics. A content package cannot promise a number of sales. After the trial, assess the quality, communication and work it has taken off your hands.
