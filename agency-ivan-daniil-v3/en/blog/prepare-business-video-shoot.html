FILMING GUIDE / JOURNAL

How to prepare your business for a video shoot

A two-hour session goes further when your space, products and people are ready. A practical guide before filming.

By Ivan & Daniil · 04.11.2026 · 3 min read

01Choose one main message02Prepare the space and products03Agree who will appear04Have brand details ready05Organise approval

Choose one main message

Do not try to show everything in a short session. Pick a service, a product or a question customers often ask. Tell us who will watch and what they should understand. That helps select useful shots rather than collecting footage without direction.

Prepare the space and products

Reserve the filming area, clean visible surfaces and make sure the products or tools are ready. Tell us about machine noise and busy periods. A small change in scheduling can make speech recording much easier.

Agree who will appear

Choose someone who can explain the work naturally. Nobody needs to memorise a long script. A short conversation and real demonstrations are a useful starting point. Confirm image permissions for staff and customers beforehand; do not assume people can be filmed without agreement.

Have brand details ready

Send a high-quality logo, correct service names and any information that must appear. Prices, promotions and contacts should be checked by the decision maker. This avoids finishing a video with an outdated offer or an incorrect name.

Organise approval

Choose one person to collect feedback and send changes in one list. Our monthly package includes one revision round. Content, copy and dates are approved before publishing. A new idea that requires another shoot needs additional scope rather than being treated as a small correction.
